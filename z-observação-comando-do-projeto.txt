sequelize:
Para criar migrations
$ pnpm sequelize migration:create --name nome-da-migration
reverter migration:
$ pnpm sequelize migration:create:undo --name nome-da-migration

Subir para o banco as migrations:
$ pnpm sequelize db:migrate
